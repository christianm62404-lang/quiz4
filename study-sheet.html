Setup & Symbols

symmeaning
Ppresent amount (t = 0)
Ffuture amount (t = N)
irate per period (decimal)
Nnumber of periods
Aequal payment / period
i = r / m        N = m · (years)
m: yr 1 · semi 2 · qtr 4 · mo 12 · day 365
  • Percent → decimal: 2.75% → 0.0275.
  • Keep i and N on the same period.
  • Don't round i early — only the final answer.
one lump sum

Single Payment

F = P (1 + i)^N        -- F given P
P = F / (1 + i)^N      -- P given F
simple interest: F = P (1 + i·N)

Solve for the unknown

N = ln(F/P) / ln(1 + i)
i = (F/P)^(1/N) − 1
double: N = ln 2 / ln(1 + i)

Round period counts up when the goal is "at least" reach a target.

compounding frequency

Nominal vs Effective

i_eff = (1 + r/m)^m − 1
  • 10% comp. monthly → 10.47% effective.
  • More frequent compounding ⇒ higher effective rate.
  • Use i_eff when the payment period (e.g. yearly) differs from the compounding period (monthly).

Two classic mistakes

  • 2.75 instead of 0.0275 → billion-dollar answer.
  • Annual i with N in months → use i = r/12.
equal payment A each period

Uniform Series (Annuity)

A at the end of each period:

F/A  F = A·[((1+i)^N − 1) / i]
A/F  A = F·[ i / ((1+i)^N − 1)]
P/A  P = A·[((1+i)^N − 1)/(i(1+i)^N)]
A/P  A = P·[ i(1+i)^N/((1+i)^N − 1)]
  • Pick the factor whose two known letters match.
  • Annuity due (pay at START): × (1 + i).
  • Yearly A, monthly compounding → use i_eff with N in years.
borrow & repay

Loans & Remaining Balance

payment:  A = P·[i(1+i)^N / ((1+i)^N − 1)]
total paid = A · N
interest   = total − P

Payoff partway through

balance = P/A on payments STILL OWED
 n = (total payments) − (payments made)

Trap: use payments left (n), not payments already made.

solve for P, N, i

Rearranging

P now for $5000 in 5 yr @20%/yr:
 P = 5000/1.20^5            = $2,009.39

$2000 → $4000 @20%/yr, how long?
 N = ln2/ln1.20            = 3.80 yr

$2000 → $4000 in 5 yr, what rate?
 i = 2^(1/5) − 1           = 14.87%
